# KPI Glossary

| KPI | Definition | Business Use |
|---|---|---|
| Visit Growth % | Change in visits vs prior period | Identify improving or declining locations |
| Conversion Rate | Orders divided by visits | Measure funnel effectiveness |
| Average Ticket | Sales divided by transaction count | Track basket size and pricing impact |
| Review Volume | Count of customer reviews | Measure feedback coverage |
| Rating Change | Change in average rating over time | Monitor customer experience trend |
